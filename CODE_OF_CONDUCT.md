# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Keytone a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, accountability and a focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, trolling, insults, threats, publishing private information or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and communication that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor. Report abuse privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.

This code is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
